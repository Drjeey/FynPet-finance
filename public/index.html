Before you use AI features

FynPet's advisor and statement import use Claude, by Anthropic. To answer you, we send:

  • your name in FynPet, monthly totals, budgets and your 10 most recent transactions (advisor)
  • the text of statements you import: dates, amounts, merchant and counterparty names (import)

Phone numbers are removed first. Anthropic processes the data to produce the answer and does not use it to train models. You can keep using FynPet without AI features.
